🏰 Forest Defense

v0.4.1
A tower-defense game you play with your legs. Hold territory, run your supply lines, and lead from the front — your bodyguard makes you the strongest unit on the field. But not an invincible one.
Session length
Difficulty
How to play: Walk/run through cells next to your territory to capture them (green). Resources only produce while connected to your Keep by an unbroken green path: 🌾 grain gives supply, ⛰️ iron (⛏️ Mine) enables veterans, 🪨 stone (⚒️ Quarry) enables walls, and a captured ⛲ spring rapidly heals troops and walls nearby. Stand by your damaged Keep to repair it with supply.

⏳ The clock is merciless. Waves launch on a fixed countdown whether or not you've cleared the last one. Win by surviving all scheduled waves and sweeping the field clean.

❤️ You can be hurt. Enemies caught in your aura stop marching and turn on you, and your bodyguard can only focus a few at a time. At zero HP they scatter — no aura, no capturing — until you retreat and recover.

⚑ Officers & bandits. Every 5th enemy is an officer who sizes up your strength: too strong to attack, and his band turns bandit — dodging your troops, razing undefended land, and massing with other bands until they're strong enough to strike. Kill the officer and the leaderless mob just charges whatever is closest.

🚩 Commanding troops: defenders engage nearby enemies on their own and return to their post afterwards. Tap 🚩 to command every defender within 60 m: tap two points and they form a line, then choose Hold ground or Engage at will (the default). Or tap a single defender, then its destination.

💪 Brutes shrug off basic defenders and smash walls — counter with ⚔️ veterans or walls + focused fire. 🐺 Wolves are fast. 🏴 Raiders hunt your economy. 🗼 Watchtowers give early, precise warning.

Victory!
