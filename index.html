🏰 Tower Defense mode (default): a steady trickle of raiders marches on your Keep from a hidden favored direction — watch where they come from and fortify that front. Walls are your army: tap 🧱 then a cell within 60 m of you (running there IS the game) — empty ground builds a T1 palisade (🌾, archers included, holds off 1–2 attackers); tap a wall again to upgrade: T2 stone rampart (+🧱), T3 ballista bastion (+⚙️, kills brutes). Walls don't fix themselves: stand near a damaged wall to start repairs (they continue slowly after you leave, fast while you stay). Enemies flow around walls hunting gaps — seal your line. Your 🐴 knight rides at half your pace, claims land as he goes, and hunts what he meets: tap 🐴, tap up to 3 waypoints, and he runs the circuit and returns. Wounded knights (below 25%) refuse orders until healed. Mines & quarries are built in person (within 60 m of a node you control). Survive the clock to win. 💪 If the Strength add-on is on: push-ups fully heal you, 5 chin-ups summon a free knight, and a kettlebell complex clears the field and pauses spawns 2 minutes — honor system, General.

⚔️ Field Army mode (classic): Walk/run through cells next to your territory to capture them — and so do your troops: any defender or officer you send marching claims the ground it crosses. Fully encircle an area and everything inside flips to you at once. Resources only produce while connected to your Keep by an unbroken green path: 🌾 grain → supply, ⛰️ iron (⛏️ Mine) → swordsmen & knights, 🪨 stone (⚒️ Quarry) → walls, ⛲ springs heal nearby troops and walls. Stand by your damaged Keep to repair it with supply.

🐎 Your officer rides ~3× faster than troops march. Tap the 🐎 button, tap a destination, then choose: rally the troops there (he takes command of every defender within 100 m of that spot — engage or hold) or just ride & claim land. He returns to your side when done; tap 🐎 mid-ride to recall him. He can be killed — keep him off the front line. Recruit a replacement for 80 🌾.

⏳ The clock is merciless. Waves launch on a fixed countdown whether or not you've cleared the last one. Win by surviving all scheduled waves and sweeping the field clean.

❤️ You can be hurt. Enemies caught in your aura stop and fight you; your bodyguard focuses only a few at a time. At zero HP they scatter — no aura, no capturing — until you recover (fastest at Keep/spring).

⚑ Officers & bandits. Every 5th enemy is an officer. Bands too weak to win turn bandit — dodging your troops, razing soft land, massing with other bands until strong enough to strike. Pennant colors show intent: red = attacking, black = bandit, amber = mustering. Kill the officer and the mob charges mindlessly.

🚩 Commands: tap 🚩 to order every defender within 60 m into a line (tap two points), then pick Hold ground or Engage at will. Or tap one defender, then its destination — send it marching through neutral ground to paint territory.

🧱 Wall garrisons: defenders never cross your walls on their own — they press up behind them, follow attackers along the wall from the safe side, and shoot over the parapet with extended range. Enemies must break the wall to reach them.

🎖️ Leadership: troops fighting within 40 m of you strike 25% harder, and visiting your troops between fights grants a 1-minute healing morale boost. Wounded troops below 30% HP retreat on their own (🏳️) to the nearest spring or the Keep, heal up, and march back to their post.

🐴 Knights (Smithy + iron) are your quick-response force — faster than a running general. They default to following you; order them anywhere with a tap or the 🚩 line command, and set any unit to "Follow me".

🔨 Building crews: mines & quarries can be built on any node you control — in person if you're within 60 m, otherwise tap the button then the node and a crew walks out from the Keep, builds, and returns. You start with one crew; hire more; protect them.

🏹 Guard towers (stone) are static long-range point defense with heavy HP; add a 🎯 ballista (iron) and the tower snipes brutes. 🧭 Directional invasion: choose a bearing and ~90% of attacks come from that quarter — fortify one front instead of a circle.

🚶 Walking pace: choose Walking on the menu and the whole world slows to match your legs — same strategy, same challenge, no jogging required.

💪 Brutes shrug off basic defenders and smash walls — use ⚔️ swordsmen. 🐺 Wolves are fast. 🏴 Raiders hunt your economy. 🗼 Watchtowers give early, precise warning. 📜 A full battle log awaits you at the end of each game.
